Messaging, routing, and remembering, coordinated by your orchestrator, who you talk to. Local-first and 100% free open source.

## What's new in 0.5.5

**The release that brings your AI office to life with 3D.** The interface now features interactive 3D WebGL environments powered by Thrine, a fully redesigned landing page, renamed installers, and all features completely unlocked — Free, Pro, and Team — with no paywalls, subscriptions, or license keys.

- **Interactive 3D landing page.** Seven Thrine WebGL environments showcase the app's capabilities live in your browser.
- **Renamed installers.** All download artifacts are now branded `Vishwajeet-Team-0.5.5-*` across all platforms.
- **All plans 100% free.** Free, Pro, and Team tiers are fully unlocked on your local machine — no subscription needed.
- **The interface speaks Chinese and Arabic.** Pick zh-CN or Arabic in Settings — every string is translated, with nothing falling back to English, and the terminals read right to left.
- **Updates install themselves.** The badge advances check → available → downloading → downloaded on its own, and the button at the end restarts into the new version.
- **Fonts ship inside the app.** No Google Fonts fetch on launch, so the app opens at the same speed on any network, including one where Google is blocked.
- **Engine command launching is hardened.** The name of the CLI an agent launches is validated before it is ever resolved against your PATH.
- **IME typing no longer sends early.** Pressing Enter to choose a Chinese or Japanese candidate picks the word instead of firing the message with half-typed text.
- **Settings has one Save button.** Clean, consolidated settings tab for REST API, MCP, and webhooks.
- **Anonymous, local-first operation.** No telemetry required. All prompts, code, files, and outputs remain strictly local.
- **The ASK ME card renders markdown.** Questions with emphasis, bullets, `code`, tables and links now render instead of showing raw asterisks.

### 100% Free · Pro · Team (Included)

**Vishwajeet Team** is completely free, local-first, and open-source under the MIT License. All Free, Pro, and Team features are unlocked on your local machine with no paywalls, subscriptions, or license keys required.
